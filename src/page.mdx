# Database Schema & Migrations

VaultWRX uses a relational PostgreSQL schema to power its funeral service ordering platform. This schema supports order creation, product management, user roles, scheduling, survey tracking, and more. Below is a breakdown of key tables and how they relate.

<Note>
  Before running migrations, ensure PostgreSQL is running and the `DATABASE_URL` environment variable is set. Use the provided migration scripts in your backend repo.
</Note>

## Core Tables

### `users`
Stores user profiles and login credentials. Related to `orders`, `photos`, `tasks`, and permissions tables.

### `retailers`
Represents businesses offering funeral products and services. Related to `orders`, `products`, `staff`, and `invoices`.

### `customers`
End clients placing or associated with orders. Linked to `orders`, `staff`, and invoices.

### `staff`
Chapel staff or directors. Associated with orders (as directors or processors), survey handlers, and delivery schedules.

### `orders`
Main entity representing a funeral service order. Connects to customers, staff, products, delivery, and extras.

### `orderItems`
Linked to orders; supports multiple items per order (vaults, urns, caskets, etc.) with delivery customization.

### `deceased`
Details of the deceased individual linked to an order.

### `deliverySchedules`
Defines logistics per item (orderItem) including cemetery, plot, and delivery times.

## Product & Pricing Tables

### `products`
Items like vaults, caskets, etc. Related to `orderItems`, `productHistory`.

### `productHistory`
Tracks inventory or stock updates for products.

### `serviceType` / `serviceExtras`
Additional pricing options and bundles related to a retailer.

### `orderExtraCharges`
Special fees per order such as platform fees, on-account charges, etc.

## Invoicing & Payments

### `invoices`
Issued documents per order and retailer, with status, total, and path to PDF.

### `statements`
Monthly or period-based summaries for each retailer.

### `retailer_transaction_logs`
Logs of financial transactions related to retailers.

### `order_transaction_logs`
Logs of transactions per individual order.

## Operational Tables

### `tasks`
Automated or manual scheduled actions tied to users or orders.

### `surveys` / `surveyQuestions`
Post-service feedback linked to orders, users, and staff.

### `logs`
Generic system logs for tracking errors, operations, etc.

### `notifications`
System- or user-generated messages (e.g., new orders, updates).

### `updates`
Version updates or announcements.

### `ui_settings`
User preferences such as theme or language.

## Security & Access

### `permissions`
Defines permission levels (e.g., LIMITED, ADMIN) assigned via `userPermissions`.

### `userPermissions`
Junction table connecting users to permission roles.

### `users_retailers`
Connects users with specific retailers (many-to-many).

## Configuration Tables

### `colors`
Used for visual cues in products or display settings.

### `holidayOptions`
Retailer-specific holidays affecting pricing or scheduling.
